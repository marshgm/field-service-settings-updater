Load a YouTube playlist onto the big screen, then walk around the cinema in 3D.

Paste a playlist URL, a video URL, or just the playlist ID (starts with “PL”).

Controls
  • W A S D or arrow keys — walk
  • Shift — walk faster
  • Mouse — look around
  • Space — play / pause
  • N / P — next / previous video
  • M — mute / unmute
  • - / = — volume down / up
  • Esc — leave the walk and reopen this menu

Single-key shortcuts are only active while you are walking (pointer locked); they are disabled when this menu is open.

Captions: open this menu with Esc, then click the CC button on the on-screen player.
